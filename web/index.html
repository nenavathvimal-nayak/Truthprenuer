Frequently Asked Questions

How does Truthprenuer validate a startup idea?

Truthprenuer initiates structured validation sprints. Founders formulate their core problem, proposed solution, target audience, and critical hypotheses. The platform gathers empirical responses from relevant community validators while running AI multi-model analysis to produce an audit of market risks, customer pushback, and recommended pivots.

Why is validating before building essential?

Over 70% of venture failures occur due to building products for which no market demand exists. Validating hypotheses early saves months of engineering effort, preserves capital, and provides data-backed conviction.
