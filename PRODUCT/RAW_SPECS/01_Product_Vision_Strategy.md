## 1. Product Vision

**VISION-001**

> **TAEED exists to prevent founders from building products nobody wants.**

TAEED is a validation network — a platform where early-stage founders submit their startup ideas for structured community feedback, AI-assisted analysis, and evidence-based decision-making — before they commit months of engineering and capital to unvalidated assumptions.

The vision is a world where every startup idea is stress-tested before a single line of production code is written.

---

## 2. Mission

**VISION-002**

> **Help early-stage founders make better decisions faster by providing structured validation, honest community feedback, and AI-assisted analysis — replacing guesswork with evidence.**

TAEED does not promise to make startups succeed. TAEED promises to give founders the evidence and clarity to know whether their idea is worth pursuing, needs to pivot, or should be abandoned — before they run out of time and money.

---

## 3. Problem

**VISION-003**

### 3.1 Primary Problem

Most early-stage founders build products based on untested assumptions. They spend 6–18 months and significant capital developing solutions before discovering that:

- The problem they're solving is not painful enough for users to switch.
- Their target audience is not willing to pay.
- Their differentiator is not defensible.
- A competitor already owns the space.
- Their business model has a fatal unit economics flaw.

### 3.2 Why This Happens

| Factor | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Confirmation bias** | Founders seek evidence that supports their idea and ignore evidence that contradicts it. |
| **Echo chamber feedback** | Founders ask friends and family, who provide encouraging but non-rigorous responses. |
| **No structured process** | Most founders lack a systematic framework for validating assumptions before building. |
| **Validation is perceived as slow** | Founders believe validation delays progress, when in reality it accelerates it by eliminating wrong paths. |
| **Lack of access to honest critics** | Finding people willing to provide structured, honest, domain-relevant criticism is hard. |

### 3.3 Scale of the Problem

- Over 90% of startups fail. [Source: Various industry reports — Confirmed]
- The #1 reason for startup failure is "No Market Need" (~42%). [Source: CB Insights — Confirmed]
- The average failed startup spends 12–18 months before realizing the market doesn't exist. [Assumption — Needs Validation]

### 3.4 Current Alternatives (And Why They're Insufficient)

| Alternative | Limitation |
|-------------|------------|
| **Y Combinator / Accelerators** | Highly selective (< 2% acceptance). Available to very few founders. |
| **Reddit / Twitter / Forums** | Unstructured, noisy, no accountability. Feedback quality is inconsistent. |
| **Product Hunt** | Post-launch discovery tool. Not a pre-build validation platform. |
| **Startup Advisors / Mentors** | Expensive, hard to access, limited to 1-on-1. Not scalable. |
| **Customer Interviews** | Valuable but founders don't know how to structure them, and finding interviewees is hard. |
| **Lean Canvas / Business Model Canvas** | Frameworks, not feedback systems. They structure assumptions but don't test them. |

**None of these alternatives provide a structured, scalable, community + AI hybrid validation system for early-stage founders.**

## 4. Opportunity

**VISION-004**

### 4.1 Market Opportunity

| Dimension | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Total Addressable Market** | ~500M people globally self-identify as entrepreneurial. [Assumption — Needs Validation] |
| **Serviceable Addressable Market** | ~50M people actively work on startup ideas annually. [Assumption — Needs Validation] |

| **Immediate Target** | Pre-seed and seed-stage founders with a startup idea they want to validate before building. [Confirmed] |

### 4.2 Timing

- **AI maturity**: LLMs can now generate meaningful competitor analysis, risk assessment, and bias warnings at near-zero marginal cost.
- **Remote-first culture**: Founders are increasingly building in isolation and need digital community infrastructure.
- **Startup tooling wave**: The startup ecosystem is unbundling and re-bundling around specialized tools (Notion, Linear, Figma, etc.) — validation remains unaddressed.
- **Cost of failure is rising**: In a tighter funding environment, founders need to validate earlier.

### 5.1 The Two-Sided Dynamic

TAEED is a **two-sided platform**:

| Side | Role | Value Received |
|------|------|---------------|
| **Founders** (Demand) | Submit validation requests, receive feedback, collect evidence, make decisions. | Better decisions. Reduced failure risk. |
| **Validators** (Supply) | Provide structured feedback, share expertise, review ideas. | Reputation, learning, community standing, "Proof of Validation" tokens. |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The cold start problem is critical.** Without validators, founders get no feedback. Without founders posting ideas, validators have nothing to review. DOC-15 (Growth Thesis) and DOC-22 (Roadmap) must address this.

## 7. Differentiation

**VISION-007**

| Dimension | TAEED | Alternatives |
|-----------|-------|--------------|
| **Structured feedback** | Multi-dimensional structured feedback form (problem frequency, willingness to pay, pricing, missing features, overall rating) | Unstructured comments |

| **Evidence collection** | Evidence Locker with typed evidence items (user interviews, prototype tests, market data, expert reviews) | No evidence system |

| **Validation Passport** | Portable reputation for validators (verified expertise, ideas improved, trust rating) | No portable reputation |
| **Pre-build validation** | Designed specifically for validating ideas BEFORE building | Post-build feedback |

## 9. Market Positioning

**VISION-009**

### 9.1 Positioning Statement

> **For early-stage founders who need to validate their startup ideas before building, TAEED is a validation network that provides structured community feedback, AI-assisted analysis, and evidence-based decision intelligence — unlike Reddit, Product Hunt, or informal advisor conversations, TAEED offers a systematic, quantifiable validation process.**

### 9.2 Category

TAEED creates a new category: **"Startup Validation Platform"**

It is NOT:
- A social network (though it has social features)
- A startup directory (though startups are listed)
- An accelerator (though it aids startup development)
- A project management tool (though it tracks progress)
- An AI chatbot wrapper (though it uses AI)

It IS:
- A validation engine with community and AI as inputs
- A decision support system for founders
- An evidence collection and scoring platform

## 11. Strategic Principles

**VISION-011**

These principles govern all product decisions. They are referenced by DOC-04 (Product Rules) and must be respected by all downstream documents.

| ID | Principle | Implication |
|----|-----------|-------------|
| SP-001 | **Validation before building.** | The product's core message: validate first, build second. Every feature should reinforce this. |
| SP-002 | **Evidence over opinion.** | Feedback must be structured and quantifiable. "I like it" is not validation. |
| SP-003 | **AI assists, humans decide.** | AI generates analysis and suggestions. Founders make the final decision. AI never fabricates evidence. |
| SP-004 | **Honesty over encouragement.** | The platform must surface uncomfortable truths. A "your idea needs work" response is more valuable than empty praise. |
| SP-005 | **Structured over unstructured.** | Structured feedback forms > free-text comments. Quantifiable > qualitative. |
| SP-006 | **Founder privacy is sacred.** | Ideas shared on the platform are protected. No unauthorized sharing, scraping, or selling of founder data. |
| SP-007 | **Meritocratic reputation.** | Validator credibility is earned through quality feedback, not follower count. |
| SP-008 | **MVP discipline.** | Every feature must justify its existence in the MVP through direct impact on the core validation loop. |
| SP-009 | **Mobile-first, cross-platform.** | Flutter ensures iOS and Android parity. Desktop web is secondary. |
| SP-010 | **Dark mode as default.** | Reflects the builder/hacker aesthetic. Light mode is supported but not primary. |

**VISION-013**

The following are explicitly NOT goals of TAEED:

| ID | Non-Goal | Rationale |

| NG-005 | Build an accelerator program | TAEED is a self-serve platform, not a cohort-based program. |

## 15. Growth Thesis

**VISION-015**

### 15.1 Core Growth Loop

```
Founder validates idea on TAEED
→ Shares validation results (evidence-backed)
→ Other founders see structured, evidence-backed validation
→ They sign up to validate their own idea
→ They also provide feedback on other ideas (becoming validators)
→ More validators → better feedback quality → more founders
```

### 15.2 Growth Channels [Proposed]

| Channel | Mechanism | Priority |
|---------|-----------|----------|
| **Organic / Word-of-mouth** | Founders share validation results with other founders | P0 |
| **Content marketing** | "How to validate your startup idea" — TAEED as the authoritative voice | P1 |
| **Community seeding** | Engage Reddit r/startups, Indie Hackers, Twitter #buildinpublic communities | P0 |
| **AI-generated insights** | Publish anonymized validation trends and startup health reports | P2 |
| **Partnership with bootcamps / courses** | Integrate TAEED into startup education programs | P2 |

### 15.3 Cold Start Strategy [Needs Decision]

- Seed the platform with 50–100 curated validation requests from real founders.
- Recruit 20–30 domain experts as initial validators.
- Founders provide feedback on others' ideas as a condition of receiving feedback on their own (reciprocity loop).
- AI Mentor provides baseline feedback even when human validators are scarce.

## 16. North Star Metric

**VISION-016**

> **North Star Metric: Number of Validation Requests that receive ≥ 3 pieces of Structured Feedback within 7 days.**

### 16.1 Why This Metric

- It measures the core product loop: ideas being posted AND receiving meaningful community feedback.
- It is a proxy for both supply (validators) and demand (founders).
- ≥ 3 pieces ensures statistical meaningfulness of feedback.
- 7-day window ensures timely feedback (founders need fast answers).

### 16.2 North Star Target

| Timeframe | Target | Status |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| MVP launch (Month 1) | 50 validations with ≥ 3 feedback in 7 days | Proposed |
| Month 3 | 200 | Proposed |
| Month 6 | 1,000 | Proposed |
| Month 12 | 5,000 | Proposed |

## 19. Major Risks

**VISION-019**

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|----|------|------------|--------|------------|
| RISK-001 | **Cold start failure**: Not enough validators to provide timely feedback. | High | Critical | AI Mentor provides baseline feedback. Reciprocity requirement. Community seeding. |
| RISK-002 | **Low-quality feedback**: Validators provide superficial or unhelpful responses. | Medium | High | Structured feedback forms enforce depth. Helpfulness scoring. Reputation system. |
| RISK-003 | **AI hallucination**: AI generates false competitor data, fake market statistics, or misleading analysis. | Medium | High | DOC-15 (AI Rules) defines hallucination controls, confidence scoring, and human oversight requirements. |
| RISK-004 | **Idea theft anxiety**: Founders won't share ideas for fear of being copied. | High | High | Privacy controls. Content protection. Legal framework in DOC-19. Community trust building. |
| RISK-005 | **Platform abuse**: Spam validation requests, fake feedback, manipulation of health scores. | Medium | Medium | DOC-17 (Moderation). Rate limiting. Account verification. |
| RISK-006 | **Founder expectations**: Founders expect TAEED to guarantee success, not just provide validation tools. | Medium | Medium | Clear messaging. Disclaimers. See DOC-19 (Terms). |
| RISK-007 | **AI cost escalation**: High-quality AI analysis becomes expensive at scale. | Medium | Medium | Tiered AI features. Caching. Model optimization. Token budgets. |
| RISK-008 | **Retention cliff**: Founders validate one idea and leave. | High | High | Decision Intelligence keeps founders engaged. Evidence collection is ongoing. Multiple validation requests encouraged. |
